## Verified current boundary

- `models/specialist.py:Specialist` has a nullable `owner` FK, `is_verified` flag and unique slug, but no verified-person account or claim history. `owner_specialist_use_cases.save_owner_specialist_profile` assigns `owner` on creation and can create pending documents; neither action proves the specialist's identity. Migration `0054_metrostation_region_specialistspecialization_and_more.py` created Specialist, SpecialistDocument and SpecialistPracticeLocation. It did not create claim or bilateral employment records.
- `SpecialistPracticeLocation` has optional `place`, address/region and `is_active`, but no role, period or confirmation fields. Its FK to Specialist is `CASCADE`; schedules also cascade through the location. `owner_specialist_use_cases._sync_primary_location` currently deletes **all** practice locations on an online-only switch. The stage-24 contract and `migration-map.md` instead require historical locations to survive; a later implementation must replace that deletion path and verify the old rows and schedule links remain readable.
- `account_deletion._finalize_locked` unlinks `Specialist.owner` and delegates typed review disposal to `review_retention.dispose_typed_reviews`. A new verified-person FK, claim actors and employment confirmations need an explicit deletion/retention rule; merely relying on the old owner unlink is insufficient. `SpecialistReview` and its stage-22 revisions/reactions remain linked to the stable Specialist PK. Stage 24 should add identity/employment data without re-keying or merging review heads.
- `SpecialistDocument.file` currently names `protected_docs/specialists/` under the configured storage; `is_public` returns `is_published` alone. The private-media boundary needs separate storage/permission enforcement and approved certificate selection; a claim document must never become a public certificate through this flag. This report does not infer current production file location from the field name.

## Initial additive schema recommendations (before implementation)

1. Retain `Specialist.pk`, slug, owner and all review FKs. Add a distinct nullable `verified_person_user` FK to the account with a partial unique constraint on non-null user (if one account may claim only one person profile), and retain its verification decision/version. Do **not** backfill it from `owner`, email/name, `is_verified`, document upload, Place or invitation. If product policy permits one person account to manage multiple Specialist profiles, that uniqueness is a domain decision for the lead before migration; the per-Specialist verified-person slot remains at most one.
2. Add historical `SpecialistIdentityClaim` rows: Specialist, claimant FK (nullable on account deletion under the chosen retention policy), status, created/decided timestamps, deciding KidsMap reviewer, evidence reference, reason and decision version. Use a unique partial constraint for one open claim per `(specialist, claimant)` and a unique current verified-person slot on Specialist. Reviewer approval locks the Specialist and claim, rechecks claimant/account state and rejects a simultaneous winner. Historical approved claims must remain possible after a legitimate later reclaim; do not impose a blanket approved-claim UNIQUE across all time. Do not store raw identity data in public fields or report artifacts.
3. Add employment history rows keyed to Specialist plus an explicit employer Organization or standalone Place target, with role, start/end dates, invitation provenance, person-confirmed-at/by, employer-confirmed-at/by, revoked/end reason and immutable confirmation audit events. Enforce `end >= start`, explicit target shape and partial uniqueness for an active exact target/role; guard overlapping periods and target membership with PostgreSQL transaction locks and service validation. An invitation, `place_id` or `Specialist.owner` alone never sets either confirmation. When both Organization and Place are present, verify the Place belongs to that Organization at confirmation; ordinary DB `CHECK` cannot enforce a cross-table membership.
4. Preserve `SpecialistPracticeLocation` as its own historical address/visit object; introduce nullable effective dates or an append-only transition table without guessing dates for legacy rows. An online switch closes/deactivates current presentation as needed and keeps historical rows and schedules. Employment confirmation is separate from the location FK, so an old `place_id` does not become a verified employment.
5. Keep claim/employment/account-deletion references `SET_NULL` or privacy-safe historical identifiers where retention is authorized, and write a versioned deletion hook that clears personal evidence, active authority and confirmations without deleting Specialist/review/location history. Exact retention/redaction policy requires the domain/privacy owner before implementation.

Assert one winner, no partially verified employment, durable history and no granted document access to managers.
