# Stage 16 volunteer workflow and moderation hub implementation plan

**Goal:** Let volunteers submit scoped Organization, Place, Program, Activity, Group and informational affiliation proposals through existing publication and relationship services; give reviewers one filtered, versioned hub.

**Architecture:** `VolunteerPlaceRevision` remains the content candidate store and `publication.propose/review` remains its only writer. Informational affiliations use existing `OrganizationPlaceRequest` transitions. A volunteer service owns target visibility/creation and hub queries compose both sources. Templates render server-side fields, current/candidate/conflict and affected branches.
