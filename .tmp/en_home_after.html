How it works

Follow steps 1 → 2 → 3. Each step has an action button.

Step 1

🔎

Choose filters in the catalog

Set category, district, and child age.

  • Category: what exactly you need (sports, arts, IT).
  • District: to keep the club close to home.
Open catalog

Step 2

✅

Compare 2-3 options

Check price, rating, age, and reviews.

  • Check format: grid cards or list.
  • Compare conditions and choose the best option.
Compare options

Step 3

📅

Open “Details” and contact

From the card, you can call or message right away.

  • Use buttons: Call / WhatsApp / Instagram.
  • Check address and map point before booking.
